Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short: this website has no accounts, no sign-up and no database. It doesn’t collect your name or phone number itself. If you use the contact form, it prepares a WhatsApp message on your own device — nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp. Optional analytics only run if you accept them.

Who we are

This website belongs to Interior Centre, an interior design and contracting service at 21, Jubilee Ghat (opposite Bipin Park), Mymensingh 2200, Bangladesh. You can reach us on +880 1790-392377 (call or WhatsApp). In this policy, “we” means Interior Centre.

Information you give us

The contact form. The form asks for your name, phone number, project type and a message. These details are not sent to this website or stored on it. When you press “Send message”, your browser builds a pre-filled WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp (a wa.me link). The message only reaches us if you then press Send in WhatsApp. Once you do, it is handled by WhatsApp, which is operated by a third party (Meta) and has its own privacy policy.

Calls and WhatsApp. If you phone or message us directly, we see your phone number and anything you tell us.

How we use it. To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project. We don’t sell your information and we don’t use it for advertising.

Information collected automatically

  • Hosting. The site is delivered by a hosting provider, which may record standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. We don’t use this to identify visitors.
  • Google Fonts. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) when a page opens. Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request.
  • Google Maps. The site only contains links to Google Maps; no map is embedded. Nothing is sent to Google Maps unless you click one of those links, after which Google’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Cookies. The site itself does not set cookies. If you accept analytics (see below), Google Analytics sets its own cookies.
  • Browser storage. Your browser may keep (a) your analytics choice, only if analytics is switched on for this site, and (b) a short-lived note, for the length of your visit, of when you last prepared a contact-form message, so the form can’t be sent repeatedly by accident. Site editors’ browsers also keep an unpublished draft of their edits. None of this is sent to us.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

Analytics is not switched on for this website at the moment, so no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is collected.

This website can use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which parts of the site and which buttons (call, WhatsApp, Google Maps, form) are used. It only loads after you click “Accept” on the cookie banner. If you click “Decline”, or don’t answer, nothing from Google Analytics is loaded.

If you accept, Google receives information such as the pages you view, the buttons you click, your approximate location, and your device and browser type, and sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<ID>. We have turned off Google signals and ad personalisation. We don’t send what you type into the contact form to analytics — only that a message was prepared, and the project type you chose. Google says Analytics 4 doesn’t log or store individual IP addresses. The data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy.

Who we share information with

Only the services described above: WhatsApp (when you send us a message there), Google (fonts, and analytics if you accept it) and our hosting provider. We don’t sell or rent your information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • You can use the site without giving us any information; calling us is always an option.
  • You can decline analytics, or withdraw your choice later, as described above. Clearing your browser’s site data also removes anything stored in your browser.
  • If you’d like to know what messages we hold from you, or want them deleted, contact us using the details below and we’ll look at your request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

This website is meant for people planning interior projects and isn’t directed at children.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No website or messaging service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please don’t include sensitive information such as financial details in your messages.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the website handles information, we’ll update this page and its “Last updated” date.
